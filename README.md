> "The machines are running. Nobody started them."

Midnight Laundry is a browser-based horror mystery game where you work the night shift in a seemingly abandoned laundromat. As strange events unfold, you'll investigate disturbing clues, monitor security cameras, survive encounters with a mysterious stalker, and uncover a dark secret buried beneath the building.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay

You are the only employee working the late-night shift.

At first, everything seems normal.

Then you notice:

- Washing machines running on their own
- Missing person posters connected to the laundromat
- Unclaimed clothing with disturbing origins
- Strange figures appearing on security cameras
- A sealed basement hiding a decades-old mystery

Can you survive long enough to uncover the truth?

---

## ✨ Features

### 🔎 Investigation System
Search different areas of the laundromat and uncover clues.

### 📷 Security Camera Monitoring
Observe multiple camera feeds and detect anomalies.

### 👤 Stalker Encounter
Hide and survive when a mysterious figure begins hunting you.

### 🔐 Puzzle Solving
Collect information and use clues to unlock hidden areas.

### 🏃 Escape Sequence
A fast-paced final chase determines your fate.

### 🌑 Horror Atmosphere
- Dynamic sound effects
- CRT security camera effects
- Screen flickers and glitches
- Typewriter storytelling
- Psychological horror elements
